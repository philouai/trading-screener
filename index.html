What this screener does

Identifies stocks showing the same setup that preceded Birkenstock's 17% snap-back in May 2026: a fundamentally strong business near multi-year lows, with a buyback signal and an imminent catalyst.

The 7 criteria

  • High short interest (>5%) — the squeeze amplifier. Shorts forced to cover magnify any catalyst.
  • Buyback signal — shares repurchased YoY. Management conviction in their own undervaluation.
  • P/FCF below 5yr average — forward PE below 85% of trailing 5yr norm. Compressed valuation.
  • Revenue still growing — positive YoY growth. Separates recoverable dip from value trap.
  • Strong fundamentals — positive OCF + profit margin >5%. Business still works.
  • Earnings within 30 days — defined catalyst horizon. Without a date, compressed can stay compressed.
  • Near 52-week low — within 20% of 52wk low. Entry point condition.

Scoring

All 7 criteria are scored independently. A stock scores 5/7 whether it hits criteria 1+2+3+4+5 or any other combination. No elimination — every stock is evaluated on all criteria.

Setup

Deploy the Cloudflare Worker (free, 100k requests/day) and paste your Worker URL in the API Setup panel. The Worker proxies requests to Financial Modeling Prep using your API key server-side, solving the CORS problem.
